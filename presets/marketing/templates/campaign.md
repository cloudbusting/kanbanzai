# <ID>: <the work, in a few words>

- **Stage:** todo (the first column; move the card only with `kanbanzai move`, never edit the Stage by hand. A card that
  cannot move keeps its column and gets a line `- **Held:** blocked: <why>` or `- **Held:** waiting: <for what>` after Where)
- **Updated:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Done:** — (the date, and where it ended up, when the card is finished)
- **Audience:** <who it is for>
- **Channel:** <where it runs: email, social, the site, print>
- **Publish on:** <YYYY-MM-DD, when it goes out>
- **Next action:** <one concrete step; the next session starts here>
- **Where:** — before work starts, then the record from `kanbanzai workspace <ID>` when it asks for one

## Goal

What this is for, in a sentence or two, and for whom.

## Message

The one thing the audience should take away, and the proof behind it.

## Acceptance checks

Observable and specific: what must be true for this to count as done.

- [ ] <check>

## Progress

A dated one-liner after each step: what was done, what was found, what changed in the plan.

- YYYY-MM-DD: <line>

## Comments

Append-only notes between sessions: a discovery, a blocker, a decision. Dated, one or two lines, never edited later.

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD — <session label>: <what the other session must know>`
